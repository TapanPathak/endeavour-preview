Copper Sulphate Pentahydrate

Also known as: Blue vitriol, Bluestone

Copper sulphate pentahydrate (CuSO4·5H2O), also called blue vitriol, is supplied by Endeavour Industries as bright blue crystals with a minimum of 24% copper and lead held to 0.003% maximum. It is used as a fungicide and copper micronutrient in agriculture, as a trace-mineral source in animal feed, and in electroplating, mining and water treatment.

Uses by industry

Agrochemicals & Fertilizers

Protective fungicide, including Bordeaux mixture, against mildew, leaf spot and blight on coffee, tea, cardamom, grapes, pepper, rubber and fruit crops; also corrects copper deficiency in soils.

Animal Feed Micronutrients

Copper source for mineral premixes and compound feed for poultry, pigs and cattle; also used in fisheries and aquaculture pond management.

Industrial Chemicals

Electroplating and electroforming baths, flotation reagent in mining, algae control in water treatment, and a raw material for other copper salts and pigments.
