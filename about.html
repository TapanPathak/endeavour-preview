Our story

From an inorganic-salts plant to a multi-product exporter

Endeavour Industries was set up in 2010 as a manufacturer of inorganic salts and agro-chemicals. Today we are a multi-product Indian manufacturer with a portfolio spanning water-soluble fertilizers, micronutrients, animal-feed chemicals, pharmaceutical salts and mud chemicals for the oil & gas industry.

Our main manufacturing facility is at GIDC Ankleshwar, Gujarat, supported by multi-location production and sourcing partners — so we can offer one of the broadest specialty-nutrient ranges from a single supplier.

  • Registered office in Amreli and branch office in Ahmedabad, Gujarat
  • Exports to South-East Asia, the Middle East, Africa, South America, Australia, New Zealand and South Asia
  • Third-party inspection by SGS, Geo-Chem or any agency the buyer nominates

Quality control

Tested in-house, verified independently

We run our own quality-control laboratory with a qualified technical team and equipment for R&D, product development and application support.

For export shipments we work with SGS India Pvt Ltd and Geo-Chem Laboratories Pvt Ltd for third-party inspection of quality and quantity — or any reputed international agency you prefer.

  • Raw-material and finished-goods testing on every batch
  • Certificate of Analysis with each lot
  • Technical data sheets for the full range
